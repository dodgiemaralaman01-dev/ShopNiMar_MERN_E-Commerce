# (An E-commerce MERN stack website selling thrift clothes to men, women, and kids)

Shop Ni Mar is an e-commerce website developed using the MERN Stack, designed to provide customers with a convenient and accessible online shopping experience for thrift clothing. The website offers a variety of clothing selections for men, women, and kids, allowing customers to explore different styles, discover new collections, and purchase affordable fashion items in one online platform.

The main objective of Shop Ni Mar is to make thrift shopping easier, more convenient, and accessible to different types of customers. Instead of physically visiting a thrift store to search for clothing, customers can browse available products, view product images and prices, explore discounted items, and add their preferred products to their shopping cart through the website.

Shop Ni Mar also includes a secure administrative panel, accessed through an admin login page, that allows the administrator to manage the website's product inventory. After signing in, through the admin panel, the administrator can add new clothing products, provide product information, upload product images, set regular and promotional prices, categorize products, and remove existing products from the product list.

The website is organized into several pages, including the homepage, men's page, women's page, kids' page, customer login page, sign-up page, and shopping cart page, as well as a separate admin login page for the administrator. It also features product collections, popular products, exclusive promotional offers, and an email newsletter subscription section.

Overall, Shop Ni Mar aims to provide an organized and user-friendly online thrift shopping platform where customers can find affordable clothing for themselves and their families while allowing the administrator to manage the store's product listings efficiently.
